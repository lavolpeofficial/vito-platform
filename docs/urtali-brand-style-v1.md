# URTALI Brand Style V1

Status: Owner-approved visual direction
Reference: URTALI SYSTEMS alpine peak artwork supplied by owner on 2026-10-20.

## 1. Brand idea

URTALI stands for clarity, orientation, transformation, development and long-term evolution.

Primary line:

> UNDERSTAND · TRANSFORM · EVOLVE

Internal German logic:

> ERKENNEN · ENTSCHEIDEN · EVOLUTION

The visual language must feel premium, calm, precise, spacious and ambitious. It should communicate height, overview and direction rather than speed, hype or technical noise.

## 2. Core visual worlds

### URTALI SYSTEMS — PEAK

Primary motif: one dominant alpine summit.

Meaning:
- orientation
- vision
- development
- excellence
- system strength
- evolution
- long-term perspective

Use for:
- URTALI homepage
- corporate and systems communication
- strategy, architecture and intelligence
- academy / leadership / transformation themes
- high-level presentations and key visuals

Avoid generic full mountain ranges when a single summit can carry the message more clearly.

### BRIDGE — PARTNER WORLD

Primary motif: elegant bridge with human connection.

Meaning:
- people
- trust
- partnership
- access
- opportunities
- handover
- market connection

Use for:
- partner portal
- recruiting partners
- scouts / sales partners
- partner onboarding
- referral / lead / opportunity journeys
- cooperation campaigns

Preferred human scene:
- one woman and one man
- smart casual / business casual
- natural handshake
- equal status
- credible, modern, non-stock-photo feel
- no ties or rigid corporate staging

Primary line:

> CONNECTING PEOPLE · CREATING OPPORTUNITIES

Secondary campaign line:

> WE BUILD BRIDGES. YOU OPEN DOORS.

## 4. Wordmark rule

The URTALI wordmark is always written with a crossbar-less A:

> URTΛLI

Rules:
- the A/Λ is the identifying mark
- the A/Λ may use URTALI Gold while the remaining letters use Navy
- do not render a normal A
- do not render a V in place of A
- keep generous tracking
- use a refined high-contrast serif for the main wordmark
- SYSTEMS uses a clean sans serif with wide spacing

## 5. Typography

Brand / large display:
- refined high-contrast serif
- generous spacing
- calm, editorial proportions

UI / operational text:
- clean sans serif
- high readability
- strong hierarchy
- no tiny metadata at 100% browser zoom

Preferred hierarchy:
1. brand / hero title
2. clear functional headline
3. concise explanatory sentence
4. metadata only where necessary

## 6. Image rules

Preferred:
- realistic alpine light
- clear atmosphere
- soft morning or evening sun
- snow / stone / sky with restrained contrast
- one clear focal point
- open negative space
- natural people when humans are shown
- warm gold light combined with cool blue-grey environment

Avoid:
- cyberpunk
- neon AI aesthetics
- black generic tech backgrounds
- busy mountain panoramas
- over-saturated sunsets
- obvious stock-photo handshakes
- artificial corporate posing
- decorative visual clutter
- excessive glassmorphism

## 7. Layout rules

- generous whitespace
- one dominant visual idea per screen
- navy provides structure
- white / ice surfaces provide calm
- gold signals priority or brand identity
- rounded cards remain restrained and functional
- shadows subtle
- borders fine and cool
- no visual competition with the hero

## 8. URTALI interface principles

The interface should feel:

> CLEAR · CALM · CONTROLLED · PREMIUM

Operational UX remains more important than decoration.

Use:
- visible hierarchy
- readable 100% zoom typography
- focused next actions
- clear status indicators
- predictable navigation
- low information noise

Do not reduce readability to preserve a decorative layout.

## 11. Do / Don't

### Do
- use one iconic peak for URTALI
- use bridge imagery for the partner ecosystem
- use Navy + Gold + Ice consistently
- keep the Λ distinctive
- prefer human credibility over corporate theatre
- use whitespace as part of the premium look
- maintain strong accessibility and readability

### Don't
- turn URTALI into an outdoor brand
- use Everest or Mont Blanc as a literal brand dependency
- use generic AI imagery
- overload pages with decorative motifs
- use multiple competing hero metaphors on one screen
- use tiny UI text
- use a normal A in the URTALI master wordmark

## 12. Master design prompt

Create a premium URTALI visual in a calm European corporate style. Use deep navy, restrained warm gold, white, ice-blue and cool alpine greys. The composition should be spacious, elegant and high-end, with one clear focal point and strong editorial typography. Render the URTALI wordmark with a crossbar-less A/Λ, preferably in gold, while the remaining letters are dark navy. For URTALI SYSTEMS use one dominant alpine peak as the main metaphor for orientation, excellence and evolution. For the partner world use an elegant modern bridge as the main metaphor for connection and opportunity; when people are shown, prefer one woman and one man in natural smart-casual clothing, equal in status, with a credible handshake. Avoid cyberpunk, generic AI imagery, heavy black backgrounds, visual clutter and obvious stock-photo staging. The overall feeling is clarity, perspective, trust, precision and sustainable growth.

## 14. Premium depth / gloss standard

URTALI surfaces should not look flat or generic. The approved visual direction uses restrained dimensionality and controlled light.

Required:
- subtle 3D depth through layered shadows
- one fine bright edge or highlight on important surfaces
- restrained metallic gold gradients for priority accents
- glass / gloss effects only where they support hierarchy
- realistic light direction and low-noise reflections
- clear separation between primary, secondary and background surfaces
- strong but elegant contrast in hero visuals
- precise edges; avoid blur that weakens the visual metaphor

For the URTALI wordmark:
- the crossbar-less A/Λ is metallic gold
- use dimensional gold with darker outer tones and a narrow highlight
- preserve clean geometry
- do not make the glyph look like a V
- do not use a flat yellow fill

For UI:
- depth must remain subtle enough for professional B2B software
- buttons may use a restrained gold metallic gradient
- cards use cool shadows and fine highlights
- dark navy headers may carry a soft reflected-light streak
- premium does not mean more decoration; hierarchy and readability remain primary

Target feeling:

> PRECISION · DEPTH · LIGHT · CONTROL
