# URTALI Partner Academy · Video Scripts V1

Status: content baseline for owner review; no recorded media yet.

## A01 · URTALI & LA VOLPE · 8 min
Goal: Partner understands the legal/brand/system architecture.
Flow:
1. LA VOLPE – Emporio Olistico is the current operating/counterparty layer.
2. URTALI SYSTEMS is the technology/system brand.
3. ADOREN = orientation, DIGITALIS = process/digitalization/automation, URTALI = custom systems, BRIDGE = people/roles/partnerships, SELECTA = curated external solutions, CARE = continuity.
4. VITO orchestrates internal processes and governance.
Closing: “Start with the decision, not the product.”

## A02 · ADOREN · 10 min
Goal: Partner understands why ADOREN is a neutral gateway.
Flow:
1. ADOREN does not begin with software.
2. Entry context is not allowed scope.
3. Business orientation can lead to NO_ACTION, INTERNAL_ACTION, deeper orientation, DIGITALIS, URTALI, BRIDGE, SELECTA or external specialist.
4. Personal evidence remains private unless separately consented and never automatically enters partner reports.
Exercise: identify when ADOREN is useful vs direct Discovery.

## A03 · Nicht Druck, sondern Zug · 8 min
Goal: Sales philosophy is operational, not just a slogan.
Flow:
1. Cold outreach creates interest, not pressure.
2. Primary CTA can be free orientation.
3. A direct conversation is appropriate when the problem is already concrete.
4. “No action” is a valid outcome.
Practice phrase IT: “Non partiamo da un software specifico. Partiamo dal processo e dal risultato che l'impresa vuole ottenere.”

## A04 · Qualification · 15 min
Goal: separate need from readiness and fit.
Explain:
- Need
- Impact
- Clarity
- Readiness
- Decision Access
- Fit
Bands: LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH.
Rule: do not sum into a false-precision total score.
Example: Need HIGH + Readiness LOW = NURTURE, not aggressive follow-up.

## A06 · Offers & Portfolio · 20 min
Goal: understand offer boundaries and authorization.
Cover:
- ADOREN Business Orientation €1.500–3.500
- DIGITALIS Discovery €2.500–5.000
- DIGITALIS Automation €7.500–25.000+
- URTALI Systems €15.000–50.000+
- CARE €500–2.500/month
Explain: partner role != authorization != portfolio permission.

## A07 · Akquise · 15 min
Goal: conduct simple, non-pushy outreach.
Channels:
- Email
- Phone
- LinkedIn
- WhatsApp only after appropriate prior contact/permission
Cadence:
Day 0 → Day 3–4 → Day 8–10 → Day 16–20 → Nurture.
Practice: Italian first-contact script.

## A08 · Discovery · 20 min
Goal: understand current process before suggesting a solution.
Core questions:
- Mi descrive brevemente come funziona oggi?
- Dove si perde più tempo?
- Chi se ne occupa?
- E dopo cosa succede?
- Può farmi un esempio concreto?
- Se cambiassimo solo lo strumento, il problema sarebbe davvero risolto?
End with one primary path and one next action.

## A09 · Pricing & Governance · 10 min
Goal: explain price without inventing discounts.
Rules:
- Partner may only use authorized price bands.
- Discounts require approved authority.
- Proposal authority is separate from signing authority.
- Margin is not exposed to partners unless explicitly authorized.
- No partner may promise delivery dates or scope outside approval.

## A10 · CRM & Lead Protection · 10 min
Goal: clean pipeline hygiene.
Every active record requires:
- Owner
- Stage
- Next Action
- Next Action Date
Lead Protection:
- starts only at ACCEPTED
- 90 days
- one approved extension
- max 180 days
Conflicts go to central review.

## A11 · Compliance · 15 min
Goal: protect customer, partner and operator.
No-go:
- manipulation of ADOREN
- unapproved discounts
- fake references
- guaranteed commercial outcomes
- medical/legal/tax claims outside competence
Data:
- minimize business contact data
- no personal ADOREN evidence in partner context
- policy updates may require re-acknowledgement

## A12 · Proposal & Handoff · 10 min
Goal: understand what happens after qualification.
Proposal:
Situation → Goal → Approach → Scope → Deliverables → Responsibilities → Exclusions → Investment → Change Rules → Acceptance.
Handoff:
Won → Project Brief → Kickoff → Delivery.
Partner remains within authorized role; delivery promises are centrally governed.
